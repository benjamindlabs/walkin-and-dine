# Restaurant Asset-Ingestion Workflow Guide

This guide establishes the repeatable procedure for bringing restaurant assets and verified facts into the Premium Website Engine.

---

## 1. Directory Structure

```text
client/
├── brief.md                 # Creative direction, dining vibe, and brand goals
├── facts.md                 # Source of truth for business information (NEVER invent facts)
├── ASSET_INVENTORY.md       # Audit register of all files, dimensions, and roles
└── assets/
    ├── raw/                 # Untouched original photography and video from client
    ├── brand/               # Vector logos (SVG), monograms, wordmarks, brand fonts
    └── processed/           # Optimized WebP/AVIF derivatives generated by the engine
```

---

## 2. Ingestion Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Ingest Client Facts & Brief
1. Populate [`client/facts.md`](file:///c:/Users/USER/Desktop/Walkin-website/client/facts.md) with verified business information:
   - Official name, physical address, phone number, operating hours.
   - Verified signature offerings with exact prices and descriptions.
   - Live reservation / ordering URLs.
2. Complete [`client/brief.md`](file:///c:/Users/USER/Desktop/Walkin-website/client/brief.md) with cuisine style, target mood, and aesthetic references.

> [!WARNING]
> **Anti-Slop & Truth Rule**: If a fact is missing (e.g. holiday hours, specific prices), leave it marked as missing in `facts.md`. Never invent filler details or make unverified dietary/award claims.

### Step 2: Deposit Photography & Brand Assets
1. Deposit client photos directly into [`client/assets/raw/`](file:///c:/Users/USER/Desktop/Walkin-website/client/assets/raw/).
   - Always preserve original files intact.
2. Deposit vector logos, icons, and marks into [`client/assets/brand/`](file:///c:/Users/USER/Desktop/Walkin-website/client/assets/brand/).

### Step 3: Run Asset Inventory & Quality Check
1. The engine logs each asset into [`client/ASSET_INVENTORY.md`](file:///c:/Users/USER/Desktop/Walkin-website/client/ASSET_INVENTORY.md):
   - Format, pixel dimensions, aspect ratio, file size.
   - Proposed layout placement (Hero anchor, Atmosphere gallery, Plated dish, Ambient texture).
   - Color temperature and contrast assessment.
2. Contrast check: If text will sit over photography, test the text color against the photo's darkest/lightest sections using `contrast-check.py` to ensure $\ge 4.5:1$ WCAG AA ratio.

### Step 4: Optimization & Derivative Generation
1. Generate responsive derivatives in [`client/assets/processed/`](file:///c:/Users/USER/Desktop/Walkin-website/client/assets/processed/) and copy production files to [`public/images/`](file:///c:/Users/USER/Desktop/Walkin-website/public/images/):
   - Desktop Hero: $2400\text{px}$ wide (WebP/AVIF, quality 85).
   - Gallery / Content: $1600\text{px}$ wide.
   - Mobile crops: Dedicated $4:5$ or $1:1$ vertical crops to prevent accidental subject clipping.
   - Next.js `<Image>` optimization enabled with explicit `priority` on the hero.

### Step 5: Transition to Build Phase
Once `facts.md` has verified identity and at least 3–5 high-quality assets are cataloged in `ASSET_INVENTORY.md`, the engine initiates the custom site build following [blueprint/WORKFLOW.md](file:///c:/Users/USER/Desktop/Walkin-website/blueprint/WORKFLOW.md).
