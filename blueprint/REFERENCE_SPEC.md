# Reference Specification — Draft 0.1

## Project
Premium Website Engine. Initial vertical: restaurants; broader goal: premium business websites.

## Visual direction
The user selected **Capiche** as the primary reference because of its combination of visual design and animation. Use it as inspiration for quality, composition, and motion—not as a template to copy. Exact implementation details have not been verified.

The El Pueblo #2 recording is a secondary reference for the business category and the creator's apparent client-asset-to-build workflow.

## Product goal
Turn a small package of client assets and verified business information into a distinctive, responsive business website with purposeful animation and functional calls to action.

## First-site acceptance criteria

### Visual design
- [ ] Distinctive hero with strong imagery and typography.
- [ ] Consistent visual identity across sections.
- [ ] Editorial layouts and varied image compositions.
- [ ] Clear hierarchy of content and calls to action.

### Animation and interaction
- [ ] Purposeful scroll-triggered reveals.
- [ ] At least one meaningful image-motion effect.
- [ ] Smooth, coherent section transitions.
- [ ] Responsive navigation and useful hover/tap feedback.

### Business experience
- [ ] Accurate business information and clear contact/ordering links.
- [ ] Coherent story reflecting the restaurant's identity.
- [ ] Usable menu or offerings section.
- [ ] No invented facts, broken links, or inert decorative controls.

### Quality assurance
- [ ] Polished desktop layout.
- [ ] Deliberately adapted mobile layout.
- [ ] Reduced-motion support and accessible controls.
- [ ] No major overflow, broken images, or console errors.

## Initial page narrative
1. Hero: brand, atmosphere, primary action.
2. Brand statement.
3. Signature offerings/menu preview, using verified items.
4. Food/interior/experience gallery.
5. Verified practical information.
6. Closing CTA and footer.

This is a starting pattern, not a mandatory template.

## Motion behaviors to evaluate
- Gentle hero image scale or parallax.
- Text/image reveals on entry.
- One coordinated section transition.
- Gallery hover/tap feedback.
- Optional pinned or scroll-linked sequence only when it improves the story.

## Unknowns
The creator's exact prompt, framework, animation libraries, timing values, and original implementation remain unverified. Our implementation choices will be based on our own tests.
