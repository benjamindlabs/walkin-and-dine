# Content and Business Facts

## Source-of-truth order
1. Verified client facts.
2. Client-approved copy and assets.
3. Clearly labeled creative suggestions awaiting approval.

Never invent addresses, phone numbers, hours, prices, menu items, ingredients, dietary claims, awards, ratings, testimonials, business history, delivery/reservation availability, or service claims.

Write concise, specific copy. Avoid generic filler and unsupported superlatives. Use CTA labels only when the destination/action is verified. Enforce copy quality using `antislop-copywriting` (eliminating empty buzzwords, significance inflation, and unverified social proof while preserving authentic narrative voice).

For missing facts, omit them, use a clearly marked development placeholder, or list them in the handoff. Never publish placeholders as facts.
