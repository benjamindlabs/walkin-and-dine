# Master Blueprint — Premium Website Engine

## Mission
Produce distinctive premium business websites through a repeatable AI-assisted workflow. Start with restaurants, then generalize proven patterns to other business categories.

## Quality priorities
1. Correct facts and functional user journeys.
2. Strong visual composition and art direction.
3. Purposeful, polished motion.
4. Responsive behavior and accessibility.
5. Performance and maintainability.

## Workflow
### 1. Understand inputs
Inspect the project, inventory assets, read the client brief and verified facts, and identify the primary business goal. Record missing/conflicting facts; do not fabricate them.

### 2. Establish art direction
Define brand mood, palette, typography roles, image treatment, layout rhythm, and motion principles before coding. Use references to understand quality, not to copy blindly.

### 3. Plan the page
Create a short page narrative. A restaurant might use a hero, brand statement, signature offerings, gallery, practical details, and final CTA. Include only sections that fit the business and available facts.

### 4. Implement
Build semantic, accessible structure. Establish layout, typography, spacing, and imagery before layering animation. Ensure navigation and CTAs work. Use responsive images and sensible component boundaries.

### 5. Add motion progressively
Implement one motion pattern at a time. Keep content readable and usable when motion is reduced or unavailable.

### 6. Inspect and refine
Run the app. Inspect desktop and mobile in a real browser. Check navigation, CTA destinations, overflow, console errors, broken assets, and reduced-motion behavior. Fix major composition and functional issues before decorative details.

### 7. Report
Summarize what was built, how to run it, checks performed, known gaps, and highest-value next improvement.

## Guardrails
- One coherent visual idea is better than unrelated effects.
- Motion should reveal, connect, emphasize, or provide feedback.
- Avoid scroll hijacking and effects that obstruct reading.
- Keep primary actions clear.
- Do not add dependencies speculatively.
